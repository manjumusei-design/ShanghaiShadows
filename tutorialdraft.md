**Player action:** `TAKE A TARNISHED BRASS KEY`

[Tutorial] There. A tarnished brass key, heavy and old. TAKE it. Items do not jump into your pocket in this city. That key is useless in the wall but in your hand it opens doors. Always take what you find. You never know what will save your life.

### Stage 11 - TAKE NOTE

**Player action:** `TAKE A CRUMPLED NOTE`

[Tutorial] There is something else beside the key. A crumpled note, the paper soft at the folds, covered in cramped handwriting. TAKE that too. Information is as valuable as gold in Shanghai. A single piece of paper can change everything.

[Tutorial] That note is covered in cramped handwriting. A message in code, meant for eyes that know how to read between the lines. EXAMINE it. A letter can hide writing between its lines.
